Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Wide format support

Open and create the archives you actually use, from .7z and .zip to .tar.xz and .iso.

.7z.zip.tar.tar.gz.tar.xz.tar.bz2.gz.xz.bz2.rar.iso

Never freezes

Long jobs run in background workers, with live progress and a one-click cancel.

Inline preview

Inspect contents and metadata without extracting anything to disk first.

Clean, adaptive UI

Follows your system theme and feels right at home on the Linux desktop.

Detailed logs

A dockable panel shows command output and diagnostics for every operation.

Speaks your language

Seven languages ship today, and community translations are warmly welcomed.
