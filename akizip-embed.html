A modern archive manager
for the Linux desktop

AkiZip is a clean, fast, and friendly tool for everyday compression and extraction. No clutter, no surprises — just a window that gets out of your way.

Features

Designed for everyday use

Every interaction is shaped around a simple goal: get your files in and out without friction.

🎨

Clean, Adaptive UI

A modern interface that follows your system theme, light or dark, and feels right at home on the Linux desktop.

📦

Wide Format Support

Open and create archives in many popular formats, including .7z, .zip, .tar, .tar.gz, .tar.xz, .tar.bz2, .xz, .bz2, .iso, and more.

⚡

Non-blocking Tasks

Long-running operations run in background workers with live progress and a one-click cancel — the UI never freezes.

🔍

Inline Preview

Inspect contents and metadata of an archive without extracting anything to disk first.

🪵

Detailed Logs

A dockable log panel surfaces command output and diagnostics, keeping every operation transparent.

🌍

Multilingual

Ships with English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, French, Italian, German, and Polish. Community translations are warmly welcomed.
